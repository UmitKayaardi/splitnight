# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-27

First public version.

- Scan a receipt (camera, file picker or drag and drop); the AI reads items, quantities, prices, tax, service charge and discounts.
- Review and fix everything inline. Includes low-confidence "Check this" chips, splitting multi-quantity lines, a reconciliation banner and undo/redo for every change.
- Full manual mode that works without any AI.
- Add friends as colourful name tags with optional emoji.
- Assign items by voice (hold-to-talk or tap mode, with a live level meter), by typing a sentence in any of five languages, by drag and drop, or with a tap-friendly sheet that supports uneven shares.
- Extras: service charge, tip (percentage or fixed, proportional or equal), discount, rounding and currency.
- Results with per-person breakdowns, totals that always add up to the bill, titles of the night, confetti, a copyable summary, a PNG summary card and native sharing.
- Bill Roulette: wheel, hidden cards, coin flip or dice, optional best of 3, three stakes, fully undoable. Just for fun — nobody is charged.
- Three fictional sample receipts so the whole app can be tried without a photo or the AI.
- Recent nights and a Hall of Fame, stored only on the device.
- English (UK), Turkish, German, French and Spanish; light, dark and system themes; installable PWA with an offline app shell.
- Privacy panel with "Clear my data"; strict Content Security Policy and other security headers.
